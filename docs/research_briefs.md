Research briefs are living documents that compile empirical data to constrain model parameters and test narrative claims.

## How briefs are used

1. **Parameterization** — EIA/IEA throughput data → `intervention` ramp magnitudes.
2. **Hypothesis testing** — Narrative claims from Game Theory transcripts are cross-referenced against official data.
3. **Model validation** — Simulation outputs are checked against empirical bounds (e.g., Hormuz flow reduction ≤ 83%).

The briefs are updated as the empirical picture evolves (new EIA reports, crisis events, etc.). Each brief is summarized in the [Empirical Grounding](empirical/overview.md) section of this site.
